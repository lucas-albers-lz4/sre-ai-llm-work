# Contradictions

Where sources disagree and how the guide resolves it.

No open contradictions at MVP bootstrap. The Assayer and contradiction-resolver
workflows append entries here as conflicting claims appear.

## Format

```markdown
## C-NNN: Short title
- **Status**: open | resolved
- **Sides**: A vs B (source-note ids)
- **Guide resolution**: …
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
```
